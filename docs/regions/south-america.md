# South America Battery Market

## Market Overview

South America is a mixed-standard market with growing vehicle fleets and import-dependent battery supply.

| Metric | Data | Source |
|--------|------|--------|
| Standards | JIS + DIN (mixed) | :material-factory: Chengguang Data |
| Top Markets | Brazil, Argentina, Chile, Peru | :material-chart-bar: Industry Data |

## Market Characteristics

- Mixed standards — JIS (Japanese vehicles), DIN (European), some BCI (American).
- Import-dependent — limited local manufacturing outside Brazil.
- Growing middle class in Chile, Peru, Colombia.

## Chengguang Presence

:material-factory: Chengguang exports to South America. 105D31 and DIN models popular. Spanish-language labeling available for OEM.
