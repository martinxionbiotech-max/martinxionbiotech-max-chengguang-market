# Middle East Battery Market

## Market Overview

The Middle East (GCC + Levant) is one of the largest import markets for automotive batteries globally. Extreme heat, large vehicle fleets, and limited local manufacturing create consistent demand.

## Market Characteristics

- Heat is the #1 battery killer — life 50% shorter than temperate climates.
- JIS standard dominates but DIN is growing with European vehicle imports.
- Large capacity batteries (65D26, 105D31, DIN100) preferred for SUVs.
