# Africa Battery Market

## Market Overview

Africa is Chengguang's largest export region — East, West, and Southern Africa with strong demand for both JIS and DIN batteries.

## Market Characteristics

- Largest and most diverse market — 54 countries.
- Rough road conditions demand vibration-resistant batteries.
- JIS dominates East/Southern Africa; DIN in West Africa.
