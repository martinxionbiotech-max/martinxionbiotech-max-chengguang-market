# Southeast Asia Battery Market

## Market Overview

Southeast Asia has a large and growing vehicle fleet, predominantly Japanese brands, making it a core JIS-standard battery market.

| Metric | Data | Source |
|--------|------|--------|
| Standard | JIS (near 100%) | :material-factory: Chengguang Data |
| Top Models | 55B24, 55D23, 65D26 | :material-factory: Chengguang Data |
| Vehicle Fleet | Japanese brands dominant | :material-chart-bar: Industry Data |

## Market Characteristics

- 100% JIS standard — Japanese vehicle dominance.
- Smaller batteries common — 55B24, 55D23 for compact cars.
- Growing middle class driving aftermarket demand.
