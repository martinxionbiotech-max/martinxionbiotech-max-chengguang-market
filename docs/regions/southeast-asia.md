# Southeast Asia Battery Market

## Market at a Glance

Southeast Asia is a predominantly JIS-standard market with a large and growing vehicle fleet. Japanese brand dominance means near-100% JIS adoption. Local manufacturing exists in Thailand and Indonesia but significant import demand remains.
