# JIS standard global map

JIS is the Japanese national battery standard, but its reach extends wherever Japanese vehicles were exported. This page maps where JIS fitments apply — and where DIN/EN or BCI applies instead.

!!! info "Data classification"
    This page is a **structural analysis** based on publicly available information about vehicle
    standards and battery technology. It contains **no market-size, growth-rate or share figures**.
    Any qualitative statement is marked *indicative*. For project-specific intelligence, contact
    Chengguang directly.

## Where JIS applies

*Based on vehicle parc origin:*

| Region | Standard family | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Japan | JIS | Home standard |
| Southeast Asia | JIS | Largely Japanese vehicle parc |
| East Africa | JIS | Japanese vehicle imports |
| Oceania | JIS (with AS variants) | Japanese vehicle parc |
| Middle East (parts) | JIS mixed with DIN | Depends on parc origin |
| Southern Africa | JIS + DIN | Mixed parc |

## Where DIN/EN applies

| Region | Standard family |
|---|---|
| Europe | DIN / EN |
| North Africa | DIN / EN |
| West Africa (Francophone) | DIN / EN |

## Where BCI applies

| Region | Standard family |
|---|---|
| North America | BCI group sizes |
| Parts of the Gulf | BCI alongside others |

## The practical point

The battery standard a market uses is a consequence of the vehicles in it — not of geography or language. For an importer, the first move is to identify the dominant standard family in the target market, then stock to it.

JIS, DIN and BCI cases are **not interchangeable** even where dimensions are close: hold-down mechanism, terminal type and polarity differ. See the [standard guides](../standards/index.md) for details.
